Support

Contact us

Email merge.kingdom.support@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days. Include your device, iOS version, game language and what happened. If it concerns your kingdom, add the friend code shown on your profile. Never send passwords or payment details.

Missing purchase or ad reward

Diamond purchases and ad rewards are verified by our server and added automatically, even after a lost connection or a relaunch. Open the game while connected to the internet and wait a moment. If a reward is still missing after a day, email us with the purchase date. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Progress on a new device

Sign in to Game Center with the same Apple account on both devices to continue your kingdom. Guest progress stays on the device where it was played until you sign in.

Delete your game account

Open Settings in the game and choose Delete Game Account. This removes your kingdom, profile, friendships and gifts. Guests can choose Delete Guest Progress. You can also email merge.kingdom.support@gmail.com to ask for deletion.

Ads and privacy

Rewarded ads are always optional. You can change the tracking choice in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking, and where required the game's Settings show Ad privacy options. Read the full privacy policy.
