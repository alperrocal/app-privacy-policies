Merge Kingdom

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Merge Kingdom handles information in its current testing version. Merge Kingdom uses Apple Game Center for player identity, Apple iCloud/Game Center saved games for cross-device progress, and Supabase for online game services. Some features require an internet connection and a signed-in Game Center account.

Information stored on your device

The game stores board state, resources, producer and kingdom progress, challenges, profile preferences, language, sound settings and local notification preferences. It also stores local daily rewarded-ad counters and a random installation identifier used for game-session coordination and save-conflict diagnostics.

Online game information

To operate online features, the game processes a pseudonymous Game Center player identifier, a temporary signed Game Center verification proof, a linked Supabase account identifier, your chosen profile name and avatar, player level, friend code, friendships, gifts, gift/economy event records and game-session/save timestamps. These records support identity verification, progress continuity, social features, security and prevention of conflicting play sessions on different devices. Your profile and friend code may be visible to players using the social features.

Apple handles Game Center sign-in. The game verifies Apple's signed proof on the server to associate installations belonging to the same player. Apple stores cloud saved-game data; Supabase stores shared profile, friendship, gift, session and related service records. We do not use Game Center verification proofs as advertising identifiers.

Optional rewarded test ads

The testing version includes Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for optional rewarded test ads. Live ad inventory and real-money purchases are not enabled in this version.

Google's SDKs contact Google to determine applicable privacy requirements and, when permitted, load and display test ads. This may involve IP addresses (including use to estimate general location), device/app identifiers and information, diagnostics, and ad interactions. Test ads do not mean that no data is processed.

The app updates consent information through UMP and requests ads only when UMP permits it. Any required consent form is presented before an ad request. Settings provides Ad privacy options when required by UMP. The current integration does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission. Ad availability and whether a privacy form appears can vary by region and prior choices.

For details about Google's processing, see Google's advertising SDK data disclosures and Google's Privacy Policy.

Notifications and your choices

Energy and task reminders are local notifications. They are scheduled only if you enable reminders and grant the iOS notification permission. You can disable reminders in the game or in iOS Settings. Language, sound and other game preferences are managed in the game's Settings. Where offered, Ad privacy options lets you revisit your advertising privacy choices.

Storage and retention

Local progress and preferences remain on your device until they are reset, removed or replaced through normal game/device functions. Cloud saves and online account/service records are stored separately and can outlast an app uninstall or a local progress reset. Reset Progress is not an account-deletion feature. We do not currently provide an automatic in-app account-deletion control; contact us to discuss access, correction or deletion requests.

Apple, Supabase and Google operate their services under their own policies and may process information in countries other than yours. Relevant provider information is available in Apple's Privacy Policy, Supabase's Privacy Policy and Google's policy linked above.

Children's privacy

Please contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information that should be removed. Do not include a child's identity or other personal information in a public GitHub issue. We will review the concern and arrange an appropriate way to discuss it.

Contact

Merge Kingdom is published by the developer using the GitHub account alperrocal. For support, a privacy question, or to request a private way to discuss an account matter, open a request through our support and privacy contact page.

GitHub issues are public. Do not post your email address, player/account identifiers, Game Center verification proofs, saved-game files, passwords or other personal information there. Ask for a private contact method first. This is the current contact channel while a dedicated support email is being established.

Policy updates and this website

We may update this policy as the app or its service providers change. The date above identifies the latest revision. The policy will be reviewed before live advertising or real-money purchases are enabled.

This policy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It includes no app analytics, advertising scripts or third-party fonts. GitHub may process technical request information when serving the website; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.
