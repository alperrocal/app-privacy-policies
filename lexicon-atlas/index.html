Lexicon Atlas

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 29, 2026 · Last reviewed: October 20, 2026

Lexicon Atlas is designed to work without advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

The app stores course selection, settings, lesson progress, word mastery, local friend-feed history and a cached Atlas Pass entitlement on your device. Local gameplay continues to work without a network connection.

Optional account and sync data

When the production sync service is enabled, Lexicon Atlas may collect an account identifier, optional name and email supplied through Sign in with Apple, learning progress, puzzle progress, friend relationships and activity, leaderboard progress, privacy preferences, and a notification device token. This data is linked to the account and used to authenticate the user and provide synchronization, social, leaderboard, notification, and security functionality.

Word-level mastery counts may also be aggregated to understand which vocabulary and exercise types need improvement. These records contain stable curriculum word identifiers, encounter and mistake counts, and completion counts by exercise type. Lexicon Atlas does not store typed answers in this analytics projection.

Usage analytics

To improve lessons, Lexicon Atlas records how the app is used: for example when onboarding, a lesson or a review starts, finishes or is abandoned, when an answer is wrong or a hint is used, when a puzzle piece is revealed, and when Atlas Pass screens or purchases are opened, completed or cancelled. Each event carries a time, an app session identifier, the app version and short details such as the course, city, lesson number, exercise type or score. Events never include typed answers, names or email addresses. When the sync service is enabled, these events are sent to it, linked to the account identifier, and kept while the account exists. You can turn this off in Settings → Share usage analytics; doing so also discards events not yet sent.

Lexicon Atlas does not use this information for advertising or tracking. StoreKit purchases are processed by Apple; Lexicon Atlas does not receive payment-card details.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. Local practice reminders can be disabled in the app. Remote notifications require permission and may use a device token stored by the sync service.

Retention and deletion

Synced data is retained while the account exists. In Settings → Privacy & account, choose “Delete account” to permanently delete the server account and associated profile, synced progress, friendships, activity, usage analytics, and device registrations. Sign in with Apple tokens are revoked during deletion when applicable. On-device learning data and Apple subscription records are not deleted by this action. Subscriptions can be managed in Apple ID settings.

Security

Authentication sessions are stored in the iOS Keychain. Network requests use HTTPS, and server records are protected by authenticated access controls.

Contact

For privacy questions or to request a private contact method, open a request in the public privacy repository. GitHub issues are public: do not post names, email addresses, account identifiers, authentication tokens, saved-game files or other personal information. Ask for a private contact method first.

This policy may be updated when app functionality or data practices change.
